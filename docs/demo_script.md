# SunReserve demo script (up to 2 minutes)

| Time | Narration and screen action |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:12 | **Problem.** “A school facilities manager may need to plan backup energy before tomorrow’s solar contribution is known. SunReserve tests whether a small radiation forecast changes that planning trade-off.” Show the app title and hypothetical-school framing. |
| 0:12-1:10 | **Set demand, edit, and run.** Before quoting any reserve value, open the assumptions sidebar and set daily demand to **25 kWh**. Keep the 15 kWh reserve cap, 20 m² panel area, 20% conversion, and 0.80 derating. In the next-day prediction for 2025-01-01, change the last radiation value from 5.7787 to 7.7787 and click **Run fresh Ridge prediction**. “This is a fresh prediction from the trained model. At the 25 kWh hypothetical demand, Ridge predicts 6.505 kWh/m²/day and plans 4.184 kWh of reserve; persistence plans 0.108 kWh and the seasonal mean 8.175 kWh. These are simulated plans from assumptions, not observed outcomes.” Show the forecast and baseline table. If you edit an observation or assumption afterward, point out that the saved result remains visible and is marked stale until rerun. |
| 1:10-1:45 | **Held-out trade-off.** In the **SunReserve mode** selector, choose **Frozen 2025 results**. “Across 365 held-out dates, radiation MAE was 0.539 for Ridge, 0.589 for persistence, and 0.712 for the seasonal mean, in kWh per square metre per day. In the separate 15 kWh/day demand and 15 kWh cap replay, Ridge’s mean simulated shortfall was 0.608 kWh/day, higher than persistence at 0.438. The equal-weight score is illustrative.” Point to the forecast-accuracy and reserve-trade-off tables. |
| 1:45-2:00 | **Limitation and next step.** “NASA POWER is a historical gridded estimate, not verified issue-time input or school meter data. Our reserve conversion is hypothetical and the app has no operational control. Next, we would check input availability and evaluate against measured site data.” |

The example inputs are historical; this demo does not show a verified live next-day forecast.
